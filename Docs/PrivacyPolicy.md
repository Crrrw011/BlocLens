# BlocLens Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [Effective Date]

BlocLens ("BlocLens", "we", "our", or "us") operates the BlocLens mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the App.

BlocLens is a climbing tool. Its social features exist only to improve route discovery, beta quality, and practical climbing records.

## 1. Who we are

- **Data controller:** Blocklens
- **Contact for privacy matters:** bloclens.app@gmail.com
- **Region:** The App is offered to users in Australia. We handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the App's core features.

**Information you provide:**

- **Account information:** your email address, a public username, and an optional self-declaration that you are 16 years of age or older.
- **Optional profile details:** height, arm span, and a regular climbing grade, if you choose to provide them.
- **Climbing records:** your private Logbook entries, including climb status, dates, attempt counts, private notes, and predicted grades.
- **Community contributions:** routes, beta links, comments, corrections, reports, votes, and other content you submit. Beta links are stored as public URL and attribution metadata only; no video files are transferred or stored.

**Information collected automatically:**

- **Authentication data:** when you sign in with Apple or Google, those providers issue an identity token that we use to authenticate you. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
- **Basic usage data:** standard technical data necessary to operate the App, such as session and account status. The App does not currently integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

**Information we do not collect:**

- Precise location data. The App's map displays gym locations but does not request or store your device location.
- Advertising identifiers.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- Operate core features: route discovery, beta contributions, Logbook tracking, and community interactions.
- Enforce account integrity (for example, the 16+ requirement).
- Respond to your requests, including privacy and support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Storage and disclosure

Your information is stored using Supabase, a third-party cloud database service. We share information with service providers only as necessary to operate the App, and require them to use it solely for that purpose.

Community contributions (routes, beta links, and comments) are visible to other users by design. Your private data — Logbook entries, follows, blocks, and votes — is never public.

## 5. Data retention and account deletion

You can delete your account from within the App. When you delete your account:

- **Private data is permanently deleted.** This includes your profile, private Logbook, follows, blocks, votes, and personal preferences.
- **Public contributions are anonymised.** Content you contributed to the community (such as routes, beta links, and comments) is retained to preserve community history, but is no longer associated with your identity and becomes unattributed.

## 6. Data export

You may export your Logbook from within the App as a CSV or PDF file. The file is generated on your device and saved by you; BlocLens does not retain a separate copy of the export.

## 7. Children

The App is intended for users aged 16 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, contact us at bloclens.app@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete it.

## 8. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to request access to and correction of the personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, or to make a privacy complaint, contact us at bloclens.app@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

## 9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the effective date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 11. Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at **bloclens.app@gmail.com**.
